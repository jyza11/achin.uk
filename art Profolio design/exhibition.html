Current Exhibition

14 May — 27 July 2026
Galerie Saint-Louis, Paris
Solo Exhibition · 22 works

Quiet Hours

A cycle of paintings made over three winters in Brittany — domestic interiors, distant tides, and the slow attention of long afternoons. Accompanied by a printed monograph with an essay by Marc Lévêque.

Curator
Hélène Boucher
Catalogue
136pp, hardback
Opening
13 May, 18h00
Admission
Free of charge

The exhibition continues at Galerie Saint-Louis through 27 July. For press enquiries or to arrange a private viewing, please write to press@eloisemarchand.fr.
