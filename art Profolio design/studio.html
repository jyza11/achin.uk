III. — On the practice

A note from the studio

Île Saint-Louis · 4° arrondissement

“I am painting the half-hour after a room has been left, when the light has not yet realised no one is there. It’s a small subject, but I think it is the only one I have.”

My work begins outside — long walks, watercolours in a cloth-bound notebook, observations of weather and water made in passing. The canvases themselves are painted slowly, in the studio, often returned to over years. I work on linen primed by hand, in glazes of warm earth and lead-tin yellow, and I keep a small palette: rarely more than eight pigments at once.

Painting, for me, is an act of attention. I am not interested in subject so much as duration — the way a room or a tide or an open window contains hours of looking, layered and partial. I hope my pictures do the same.

BornLyon, 1981
StudiedÉcole des Beaux-Arts · Slade
